## Directly supported source meaning

1. SP2L is the author's name for Spike → 2 Leg.
2. Spike is contextual: a sharp/strong movement after a range, not simply an arbitrary large candle.
3. Breakout and follow-through are part of the spike discussion.
4. P-GAP is explicitly associated with the breakout/spike concept.
5. P-GAP is explicitly distinguished from E-GAP and Common-GAP; therefore a generic three-candle imbalance must not automatically be called P-GAP.
6. Multiple spike constructions are intentionally treated as the same strategy concept, although the author notes they could later be separated.
7. 2Leg is explicitly described as AB=CD.
8. A correction is expected after the spike before the second leg.
9. The second leg is expected to equal the first leg.
10. Pending-limit execution is explicitly demonstrated during the correction setup.
11. Return to the relevant structural invalidation area invalidates the scenario.
12. Stop placement is structural; pending orders may be deleted/replaced according to SL distance.
