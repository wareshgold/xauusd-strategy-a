# G370 — Hypothesis Discrimination Plan

## Objective
Run the G369 minimal pairs against the G368 registry and classify each hypothesis as:

- **survives** — no contradiction exposed;
- **distinguished** — fixture produces a materially different output;
- **rejected** — contradicts an explicit source fact or fixture invariant;
- **underdetermined** — competing hypotheses remain observationally equivalent.

## Critical constraint
A `survives` or `distinguished` result is not source confirmation. Synthetic evidence can expose consequences and contradictions only.

## Required outputs
1. per-fixture hypothesis output matrix;
2. contradiction log;
3. observational-equivalence groups;
4. unresolved-dimension summary;
5. explicit recommendation for whether another primary-source hunt is justified.

No historical optimization is part of G370.
