Date: 2026-09-13

## Gate decision

**DEV HARNESS READY; CANONICAL STRATEGY DEV BLOCKED.**

G386 prepares reproducible infrastructure without implementing any unresolved Strategy A geometry.

## Included

- explicit temporal DEV / VAL / HOLDOUT split assignment;
- dataset manifest carrying symbol, provider, timezone, timeframe, version and coverage;
- deterministic spread/slippage/commission cost plumbing;
- deterministic R calculation after costs;
- hard assertion that canonical execution is not authorized.

## Deliberately excluded

G386 does not define or infer:

- P-Gap endpoints or formula;
- spike strength thresholds;
- A/B/C/D anchors;
- correction-selection geometry;
- pending-limit price or fill semantics;
- SL boundary/offset;
- TP mapping;
- session or candle filters.

## Validation policy

DEV and VAL are chronological research partitions. HOLDOUT is isolated by the same interface but must remain untouched until the strategy specification is frozen.

The harness is therefore an infrastructure layer, not a trading strategy and not evidence of profitability.

## Promotion gate

No canonical Strategy A trade generation is permitted until Frozen Geometry is resolved and the source-aligned specification passes the required fixture and validation gates.
