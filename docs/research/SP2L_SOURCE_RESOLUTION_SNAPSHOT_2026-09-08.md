## 3. Source-confirmed semantic findings

### Strategy identity

- SP2L is the author's naming for the Spike → 2 Leg concept.
- The name is a strategy identifier, not evidence for any extra technical indicator.

### Context / Spike

- Spike is described as a strong/sharp directional movement after a range/context.
- The source discusses candle-level OHLC language and structural reading.
- The source distinguishes channel-like overlapping movement from the spike/breakout construction.
- Multiple spike constructions are treated as conceptually equivalent for this strategy, although the author notes they could theoretically be separated later.

### Breakout / Follow-through

- Breakout is explicitly part of the spike construction.
- Around 30:53–31:18 the source describes a candle closing beyond a prior level and the next candle acting as follow-through/key bar without returning into the prior range.
- The source later describes variants where higher lows precede the gap/breakout; these are still treated as the same spike concept.

### P-Gap

- The source explicitly states/displays the relationship **Valid BO = P-Gap**.
- P-Gap is explicitly distinguished from E-Gap/Common-Gap; not every visible gap is the same.
- Around 34:25 the source explains a gap where a referenced high and low do not overlap, associated with breakout + follow-through.
- The official source page also states that a valid Spike requires a P-Gap and that a sharp movement without the required gap is invalid.

**Important:** this is semantic confirmation only. The exact executable P-Gap candle indices, boundaries, and formula are still unresolved.

### Two-leg / AB=CD

- The source explicitly displays **AB = CD**.
- The source explains that after the first movement, correction is expected and a second leg of equal/same magnitude is expected.
- The transcript explicitly connects SP2L / 2Leg with AB=CD.
- The source adapts the AB=CD idea to candle-level work rather than requiring a generic internet/classical implementation.

### Correction / Entry

- Correction follows the Spike before the second leg.
- Around 38:38–39:26 the source explicitly says the order can be placed manually or as a **pre-set Limit** during correction.
- A direct `Buy Limit` annotation is visible in the source video.
- The source therefore does **not** authorize replacing pending-limit entry with a later market-close reclaim trigger.

### Stop / Invalidation

- Structural invalidation is explicit.
- The source explains that if price returns to the relevant invalidation area, the scenario is invalid.
- Stop placement is structural and the source discusses deleting/replacing pending orders when the risk distance changes materially.
- No discretionary SL widening is to be invented.

### Targets / 2X

- The educational video shows Entry / SL / TP1 / TP2 and a separate `2X` module.
- The official source page states default TP of 1:1 and also describes a secondary/add-on concept.
- The real execution examples show multiple entries and management behavior that cannot safely be collapsed into one universal fixed-target formula.
- `2X` remains a distinct source module until its exact relationship to core SP2L target management is resolved.
