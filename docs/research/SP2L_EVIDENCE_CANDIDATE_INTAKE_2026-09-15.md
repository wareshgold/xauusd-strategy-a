# SP2L Evidence Candidate Intake Gate — 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Record new source evidence without allowing the engineering layer to invent or silently promote Strategy A geometry.

## Candidate dispositions

- `REPEAT_ONLY`: evidence repeats already-supported source semantics and does not discriminate executable geometry.
- `NARROWS_NOT_UNIQUE`: evidence reduces the hypothesis space but does not uniquely determine an executable rule.
- `READY_FOR_MANUAL_ADJUDICATION`: Tier-1/Tier-2 evidence makes a materially different hypothesis state reviewable by a human adjudicator.

`READY_FOR_MANUAL_ADJUDICATION` is **not** canonicalization and does not authorize execution.

## Required provenance

Every candidate requires an artifact reference, source tier, timestamp, and at least one frame or transcript reference.

## Required discrimination record

Every candidate records:

1. target geometry dimension(s);
2. explicit discriminator claim;
3. before-hypothesis state;
4. after-hypothesis state;
5. disposition.

## Governance

This gate contains no P-Gap formula, entry anchor, fill semantics, structural invalidation boundary, pending-refresh threshold, trigger classifier, AB=CD anchors/tolerance, TP1/TP2/2X formula, buffer, or BUY/SELL logic.

Backtest performance and optimization cannot change a candidate disposition. Geometry remains blocked until source evidence is genuinely discriminating and manually adjudicated.

## Current status

The intake contract is infrastructure only. It does not change the current source-resolution stop or frozen-geometry readiness result.
