# SP2L G56 — Source Discrimination Matrix — 2026-09-09

## Purpose
G56 turns the G55 candidate registry into an explicit source-discrimination matrix. The matrix records what the source supports, what remains only a candidate, and what blocks executable Strategy A geometry.

## Findings
- **B1 P-Gap:** source concept is supported; competing boundary constructions remain unresolved. Generic three-candle FVG is not canonical.
- **B2 Entry:** pending Limit and relevant structural Low/High semantics are supported. A latest-HL/LH construction is visible in examples but is not promoted to universal geometry.
- **B3 SL:** structural invalidation is supported. Exact wick/body/OHLC boundary remains unresolved.
- **B4 Trigger:** one-, two-, three-candle and Key-Bar forms are source-consistent. Exact acceptance, timing, and precedence remain unresolved.
- **B5 AB=CD:** magnitude relationship is supported. Wick/body/pivot/mixed endpoint models remain candidates; no tolerance is invented.
- **B6 Leg2:** continuation and approximate/equal magnitude relationship are supported. Executable projection and TP geometry remain unresolved.

## Decision
G56 does **not** freeze geometry. Production remains blocked while critical source geometry is unresolved.

## Research rule
A source-consistent candidate is not equivalent to a source-proven canonical rule. Synthetic fixtures can falsify candidates but cannot manufacture missing source evidence. Backtest performance is excluded from the meaning-selection decision.
