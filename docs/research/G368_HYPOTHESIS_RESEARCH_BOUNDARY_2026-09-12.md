# G368 — Hypothesis Research Boundary

## Allowed
- Construct deterministic candidate interpretations.
- Generate synthetic and adversarial fixtures.
- Measure whether candidates produce different outputs.
- Record rejection or survival as a research result.

## Not allowed
- Calling a surviving hypothesis canonical.
- Choosing geometry because it maximizes profit.
- Using third-party code as authoritative evidence.
- Introducing liquidity sweeps, BOS/MSS, displacement, FVG, retest, or session gates as canonical rules without source evidence.
- Beginning production/live signal generation.

## Required next step
G369 should focus on minimal-pair adversarial fixtures that isolate one unresolved dimension at a time: P-Gap endpoints, A/B/C/D anchors, parent/nested scale, entry/fill distinction, SL boundary, and target mapping.
