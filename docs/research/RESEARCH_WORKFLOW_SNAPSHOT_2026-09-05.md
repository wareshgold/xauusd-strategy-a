## 1. Research principle

Strategy A is research-first and deterministic. No BUY/SELL decision is generated by AI. Production changes require statistical evidence and must never be promoted from a diagnostic finding alone.

Research order:
1. Define rules precisely.
2. Run reproducible historical research.
3. Split development / validation / fresh holdout chronologically.
4. Measure expectancy, PF, WR, drawdown, AvgR, consecutive losses, and path/session behavior.
5. Reject unstable hypotheses.
6. Freeze a hypothesis before Fresh Holdout evaluation.
7. Promote only after robustness evidence.

## 3. Baseline Strategy A chain

`breakout → follow-through → spike → first correction → entry trigger → leg2 projection → invalidation → EMA/location/session context → quality score → RR directional checks`

Canonical trade mechanics:
- canonical stop = first-correction extreme
- BUY stop = correction low
- SELL stop = correction high
- trigger = first post-correction candle whose close reclaims the correction extreme
- entry = trigger candle close
- same-candle SL/TP = AMBIGUOUS
- baseline `decide()` returns at most one candidate

Baseline context includes EMA60, round-step/location checks, London 07–16 UTC, NY 13–22 UTC, and no avoid windows in the current baseline.

## 6. Current finding

The conditional-path artifact confirms a strong relationship between post-entry adverse excursion and final canonical R, especially on 5m.

The relationship is directionally stable across the validation windows. 1m is weaker but remains directionally consistent.
